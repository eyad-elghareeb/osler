# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 1 September 2026  
**Platform:** Osler Medical Study Platform

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Osler ("the Platform", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Osler Medical Study Platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All study progress, session state, and configuration is stored **locally on your device** using the browser's IndexedDB. This includes:
- Study progress (questions answered, flashcard intervals, scores)
- Session data and authentication tokens
- App preferences and settings
- Spaced repetition schedules

This data **never leaves your device** unless you enable cloud sync.

### 2.2 Account Data (Cloud Mode Only)

If you create an account and enable cloud mode, we store:
- **Username and display name** — for identification
- **Email address** (optional) — for account recovery
- **Hashed password** — we never store plaintext passwords
- **Study progress** — synced to our servers to enable cross-device access
- **Audit logs** — records of admin actions (admin accounts only)

### 2.3 Support Tickets

If you submit a support ticket, we store the content of your message and any related context (e.g., a question ID). This is used solely to respond to your request.

### 2.4 Usage Analytics (Optional)

The Platform may collect anonymised usage telemetry (page views, engine usage frequency) if you opt in via Settings. No personally identifiable information is included in these events.

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not:
- Use third-party tracking cookies
- Sell your data to third parties
- Use advertising networks
- Track you across other websites
- Use Google Analytics or similar external analytics services

## 4. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---|---|
| Delivering study features | Local IndexedDB — never transmitted |
| Account authentication | Username, hashed password, token |
| Cross-device sync | Study progress, preferences |
| Responding to support requests | Ticket content |
| Platform security & integrity | Audit logs (admin accounts) |

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except:
- **Service providers** (e.g. Cloudflare, our hosting infrastructure) who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements
- **Legal requirements** — if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local data**: Persists on your device until you clear it via Settings → Data or clear your browser storage.
- **Account data**: Retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.
- **Support tickets**: Retained for up to 12 months after resolution.
- **Audit logs**: Retained for up to 24 months for security purposes.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Portability**: Export your study data via Settings → Data → Export

To exercise these rights, use the data export/delete tools in Settings, or contact us via a support ticket.

## 8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- All data in transit is encrypted via TLS
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage
- API tokens are stored as hashes (the plaintext is shown only once at creation)
- Biometric authentication is supported via WebAuthn (credentials never leave your device)

## 9. Cookies and Local Storage

Osler uses:
- **Browser sessionStorage / localStorage**: For authentication tokens and session state
- **IndexedDB**: For study progress and app data
- **No third-party cookies**

A one-time consent notice is shown on first use. This notice itself is stored locally; no consent record is sent to our servers.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice. Continued use of the Platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

## 12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights:
- Use the support ticket system within the Platform
- Contact us at the email listed in Settings → About
